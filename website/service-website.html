Common questions

Before you decide

How quickly can you start?
We take on a limited number of clients each quarter. Once your deposit clears, we schedule the kickoff session within 48 hours. Current availability is confirmed on the discovery call.
What do you need from me to get started?
Your time for the kickoff session (1–2 hours), prompt feedback within 5 business days at each review stage, and honest reactions — not polite ones. The more direct you are about what works and what doesn't, the better the output.
Can we scope this down to fit a smaller budget?
We can reduce scope — not rate. A smaller scope means fewer deliverables or a shorter timeline, with a clear understanding of what's being deferred. We'd rather do less properly than more poorly. Book a discovery call and we'll work out what the right scope is for where you are.
