The problem with most brand design

A logo is not
a brand.

Most businesses get a logo. Some get a colour palette. Few get a complete visual identity system — and almost none get one that was built on a strategic foundation.

The result is a visual presence that looks inconsistent across channels, dates quickly because it follows trends rather than truth, and fails to communicate anything specific about the business behind it.

"Identity built without strategy is decoration. Identity built on strategy is architecture."
— Zareya, The Illumination Process

Common questions

Before you decide

Can I get just a logo — without the full system?
We don't offer standalone logo design without the surrounding system. A logo in isolation isn't a brand — it's a symbol without context. The colour palette, typography, and usage guidelines are what make a logo function consistently across your website, social media, print, and signage. Without the system, you'll be back asking a designer for help every time you need to produce new materials.
How many logo concepts will I see?
We present 2–3 clearly differentiated directions, each grounded in the brand strategy rationale. We make a recommendation and explain why. Then you select a direction and we develop it fully. We don't show you ten versions and ask you to pick — that approach produces average results because it's driven by preference, not strategy.
What file formats will I receive?
AI, EPS, SVG, PNG (transparent background), JPG, and PDF. Delivered in both full-colour and white/black versions. Organised in a clearly labelled Google Drive folder so you can find what you need in seconds rather than hunting through files.
What if I need changes after delivery?
The 30-day support period covers minor refinements and questions. Because the identity is built on agreed strategy, major revisions are rare — the direction is confirmed before any design begins. Significant changes outside the agreed scope are quoted separately.
