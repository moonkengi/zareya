Our methodology

The Illumination
Process

Every project we take on follows the same rigorous methodology. Four phases. Zero guesswork. One standard: the work must illuminate something true about your brand.

The problem

Most brand work
starts in the
wrong place.

A designer opens Illustrator. A marketer starts writing copy. A social media manager starts posting. Nobody asked the important questions first.

What does this brand actually stand for? What does the customer feel when they interact with it? What is the one true thing this business can say that no competitor can honestly say?

Without those answers, everything that follows is decoration. Beautiful, possibly. But built on nothing.

Four phases. One brand.

The process is the
product.

01
Discovery & Immersion

We go deep before we go wide. Two weeks of research — your customers, your competitors, your category, your cultural context. We interview real people. We study your brand's world until we understand it better than you do.

We don't start any creative work until discovery is complete. Every insight becomes a strategic asset. The output is a Discovery Report: everything we found, everything it means, and what it changes about how your brand should show up in the world.

📋 Deliverable: Discovery Report
02
Strategy & Positioning

With discovery complete, we build your brand's strategic foundation. Where you play, how you win, what you stand for, who you are for. We define your brand positioning, personality, voice and tone, and long-term narrative arc.

Strategy is presented in a single document — clear, decisive, no fifty-page decks with hedged language. If we can't explain your positioning in one sentence, we haven't done our job. You get a document you can hand to any new team member and they'll understand your brand in thirty minutes.

📋 Deliverable: Brand Strategy Document
03
Creative & Execution

Strategy becomes work. We build the visual identity, campaign concepts, content, copy — everything the brand needs to live in the world. We work fast and we work precisely. One revision round, not ten. We present fully formed work, not directions. You see the real thing.

Our creative philosophy: distinctiveness over beauty. A brand doesn't need to be beautiful. It needs to be unmistakable. We design for recognition, not admiration. The result is a brand that people remember, not one they merely appreciate.

📋 Deliverable: Brand Identity System + Campaign Assets
04
Launch & Amplification

We don't deliver files and disappear. We manage the launch, run the first campaigns, optimise the first 90 days. Then we hand over a brand that's proven, not just designed. Real data, real customers, real performance — not a beautiful PDF that sits on a shelf.

Every engagement ends with a full Brand Handbook — your team can run everything forward independently. We also provide a recorded handover session with every decision explained so the logic lives with your team forever.

📋 Deliverable: Brand Handbook + 90-Day Launch Report
