About Zareya

Built for Africa's
next generation
of brands

Zareya was founded in Nairobi with a simple belief: African businesses deserve brand partners who understand their markets from the inside out.

We are not generalists. We specialise in ambitious, growth-stage businesses — startups raising capital, established companies re-entering the market, and regional brands expanding across the continent. Our team is small by design. Every client we take on gets our best thinking, not a templated process.

What we believe
Clarity over complexity
Good strategy fits on one page. If we can't explain it simply, we haven't understood it deeply enough. Every brief, strategy, and campaign must be explicable in a conversation. If it needs thirty slides to make sense, it doesn't work.
Context is everything
What works in London or New York does not automatically work in Nairobi. The consumer insight, the cultural reference, the media behaviour — it's different. We build for our market first, with the rigour of any world-class agency.
Craft matters
Details are not details. The choice of typeface, the shade of colour, the rhythm of a sentence — these things add up to everything. The quality of the work is the quality of the agency. There is no gap between the two.
Small is intentional
We cap the number of clients we take on each quarter. This is not a growth strategy — it's a quality commitment. Every client deserves our full attention. When you work with Zareya, you're working with Zareya.
Honest over comfortable
We will tell you the truth about your brand, even when it's uncomfortable. We will tell you if your idea is wrong. We will push back when something conflicts with your stated goal. That honesty is the service. Agreement without challenge is not partnership — it's just compliance.

Our foundation

Built inside a

Zareya is the exclusive brand and marketing partner of Pulse Group — a Kenyan technology conglomerate operating across internet, fintech, media, and more.

That relationship gives us deep, practical experience building brands from zero, at scale, under real business pressure. We understand African consumers, African business culture, and African digital behaviour because we operate inside it — not as outside consultants observing from a distance.
